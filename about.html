About Solarcentric Technologies

A project development and energy services company with over 10 years’ experience. We work with both local and foreign partners (established) to support projects with the main objective to deliver value to end clients.

To promote and provide affordable & efficient energy and integrated management services thereby improving operational profits of our clients.

Our Mission

To become a dominant indigenous energy services provider and and a reference point for both power supply and integrated management services.
